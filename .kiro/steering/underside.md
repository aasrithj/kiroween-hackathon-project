# The Underside: escape the Devil's party before 3:00 AM

After the red-neon title intro (80s supernatural feel), the visitor presses Go deeper and enters an upside-down house. Gentle mode has no button: it turns on automatically for visitors who prefer reduced motion. The host of Nocturne Night is the Devil. The party ends at 3:00 AM, the devil's hour. The visitor must open every lock and get out before then.

## Feel and pacing
- The build-up is slow. It opens ordinary-made-wrong: an empty party hallway, muffled synth, nobody there. Nothing jumps for a long while.
- It rots from 80s red neon and a synth arpeggio into VHS horror (drones, dark blood) as `dread` rises. `--neon` in CSS and the synth bus in `audio.js` follow dread.
- Scroll UP to go deeper, slowly (speed-capped). The opening card says so.

## Rooms and locks (`BEATS` and `GATE_AT` in main.js; a lock blocks scrolling until solved)
1. **hall**: 3D portrait corridor (`gallery-hall.js`). Lock: click the host's portrait (Doré's Lucifer).
2. **tape**: 3D CRT living room (`crt-room.js`). Lock: press STOP three times, then EJECT. One tape only.
3. **nursery** (`doll.js`): porcelain doll and music box. Lock: hold to wind, three times. The doll changes and comes closer each time; looking away while it plays is punished.
4. **eyes**: "We see you."
5. **rooms**: repeating doors and a radio search party (voices). Lock: choose one of three doors. Wrong door brings the hammer.
6. **leave**: "Are you sure you want to leave the party?" Stay / Stay; the × dodges your cursor.
7. **hallway**: the figure closes in between cuts; climax: it grows from the corners, cuts off its arm, offers it ("Take it"), then its face.
8. **devil** (`devil.js`): Lock: type the name you signed up with to cancel your RSVP. A wrong name brings "Wrong", the hammer, and −30 s on the clock.
9. **exit**: hold to escape (pointer, or hold Space). The "You got out." page appears, and after a few seconds a final scare flashes on it once (never in gentle mode).

If the party clock reaches 3:00 AM first, "The party has ended." plays a long finale (corner creep, severed-part rain, the cake stab scene, gore scenes, hands on glass, the Devil's claw, the hammer, a blood flood, then its face). In gentle mode it shows the text only and returns to the invitation.

## Overlays (`overlays.js`)
- Heartbeat: red corners pulse at a fear-driven BPM; the audible heartbeat follows it.
- Blood: drips from the top as dread rises; splatter wherever a splat sound plays. Always dark red, never a bright flash.
- Cracks: the screen breaks where the hammer lands.

## Rules
- Every flash and scare goes through `createFlashLimiter` (≤ 3/s) and `createScareGate` (≤ 2 jumpscares/run; the time-out ending may force one, never in gentle mode). The hammer counts as a flash.
- Gentle mode / reduced motion: no jumpscares, hammer or flashes; quieter audio; extra time.
- All copy in `www/js/content.js` (`underside`); cards ≤ 8 words (tested).
- Images: public domain / CC0, or generated by the team (ASSETS.md). Never ship the third-party images in `CreepyImages/`.
- Voices: ElevenLabs via Higgsfield in `www/assets/underside/voice/`, processed live; browser speech is the fallback.
- The party clock is 10 minutes (`timeLimitMs`, 2:50 AM to 3:00 AM); gentle mode adds 2 minutes (`gentleExtraMs`).
- `?time=SECONDS` sets the clock (10 to 900 seconds) for demos; `?debug` exposes `window.__underside` for automated walkthroughs; `?descend` skips the invitation and starts at the title sequence.

## One page
Everything lives in `www/index.html`: the invitation, then (after the third RSVP) the canvas title sequence and the Underside. There is no separate arrival screen. The crossing stays on the page and calls `beginTheOtherSide()` in `www/js/arrival.js`, which starts the title sequence and the music autoplay attempt with its sound toggle, and clears leftover transforms on `<html>`/`<body>` (a transform there traps every fixed full-screen layer). Go deeper hides the sound toggle and fades the music out. `www/escape.html` only plays the title video and continues to `./index.html?descend`.

## Set-pieces
- 2D canvas: hammer (three blows, keeps smiling), arm offer + bone stab, doll head roll, hands on glass (real hand image), Devil's claw scratches, severed-part rain, blood flood, corner faces.
- 3D (three.js): cake stab with a scanned head (`stab-scene.js`), and low-poly gore scenes in `massacre.js`: throat, hand, stabline, gut, bugface, facewall. Stylised low-poly on purpose; all skipped in Gentle mode.
- Text (cards, prompts, timer, radio) always sits above blood/cracks/scares (z-index 30). Blood, cracks and scratches clear within a few seconds.

## Performance
Adaptive quality lowers canvas resolution when frames run slow; static noise uses cached tiles; `--dread` updates only in 2% steps; the heartbeat corners animate opacity only. Avoid per-frame shadowBlur, per-pixel loops and CSS variables that repaint full-screen gradients.
