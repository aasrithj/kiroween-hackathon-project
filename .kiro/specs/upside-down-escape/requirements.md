# Requirements Document

## Introduction

Upside_Down_Escape is the other side of Nocturne Night: everything that happens after the third valid invitation submission. It runs on the same page as the invitation (`www/index.html`) and has three parts:

1. **The title sequence.** A canvas animation spells NOCTURNE NIGHT in red neon while the arrival music plays. It cuts between slow close-ups of the letters, pulls back to the full title, and then holds it indefinitely. A short message and a Go deeper button fade in underneath.
2. **Go deeper.** The signup's glitch and strongest shake play over the title, and the screen turns over into the Underside.
3. **The Underside.** An upside-down house, the Devil's party, which the visitor must escape before 3:00 AM. The visitor scrolls up to go deeper through nine rooms, opens six locks, and holds to escape. If the party clock reaches 3:00 AM first, the party ends with a long finale.

The visual direction is an original 1980s supernatural mood that rots from red neon into VHS horror. Copy, puzzles, and the title treatment are original. Images are public domain or generated by the team, and the voices were recorded for the project.

The experience uses HTML, CSS, plain JavaScript ES modules, GSAP, three.js (vendored), Canvas 2D, Web Audio, and speech synthesis, with an optional Spline runtime. It has no web UI framework or bundler. Capacitor 8 packages the same `www/` folder for Android and iOS, and GitHub Pages hosts it.

This spec replaces the earlier plan for a seven-minute "Trench of Horror" clue hunt, which was not built.

## Glossary

- **Site**: The Nocturne Night invitation and the Upside_Down_Escape, both in `www/index.html`.
- **Invitation**: The landing and signup page specified in `.kiro/specs/spooky-haunting-ui/`.
- **Other_Side**: The part of the Site that starts after the third valid submission: Title_Sequence, then the Underside.
- **Title_Sequence**: The canvas animation of the NOCTURNE NIGHT neon title (`www/js/title-sequence.js`).
- **Finale**: The message and Go deeper button shown under the settled Title_Sequence.
- **Arrival_Music**: The local music file (`www/audio/kiroween-audio.mp3`) and its sound toggle.
- **Underside**: The upside-down house the visitor must escape (`www/js/underside/`).
- **Beat**: One of the nine rooms of the Underside, in order: hall, tape, nursery, eyes, rooms, leave, hallway, devil, exit.
- **Lock**: A puzzle that blocks scrolling past a point in a Beat until it is solved.
- **Party_Clock**: The countdown from 2:50 AM to 3:00 AM shown during the Underside.
- **Dread**: A value from 0 to 1 that rises as the visitor goes deeper and drives color, sound, and overlays.
- **Gentle_Mode**: The Underside's safe mode, enabled when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
- **Jumpscare**: A brief full-screen scare image.
- **Flash**: Any sudden bright change, including the hammer.
- **Outside_Page**: The "You got out." screen shown after a successful escape.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Technology and Runtime Constraints

**User Story:** As a developer, I want the other side to share the project's small static stack, so that it runs anywhere without a framework or build system.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Upside_Down_Escape SHALL use only HTML, CSS, plain JavaScript ES modules, browser APIs (Canvas 2D, WebGL, Web Audio, speech synthesis, sessionStorage, requestAnimationFrame, matchMedia), GSAP, and three.js for runtime behavior.
2. THE Upside_Down_Escape SHALL load three.js and its addons only from the vendored files in `www/vendor/three/`.
3. THE Upside_Down_Escape MAY load the Spline runtime from its pinned unpkg URL, and SHALL load it only when a scene URL is configured in `SPLINE_SCENES`.
4. THE Upside_Down_Escape SHALL NOT depend on a web UI framework, CSS utility library, bundler, game engine, or server API.
5. THE Upside_Down_Escape SHALL keep its pure rules (title timing and camera, Underside beats, dread, clocks, gates, scroll limits, flash and scare limits) in `www/js/logic/` without DOM, GSAP, or audio dependencies.
6. WHEN packaged for native platforms, THE Site SHALL keep its editable web app in Capacitor's `www/` web directory and synchronize it into the Android and iOS shells through the documented npm scripts.

### Requirement 2: Title Sequence

**User Story:** As a visitor who has just crossed over, I want a cinematic title reveal, so that the change from the invitation feels like the start of a story.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Other_Side begins, THE Site SHALL hide the invitation and show the Title_Sequence full-screen, edge to edge.
2. THE Title_Sequence SHALL start on 2 seconds of black, fade in over 1.6 seconds, and show five close-ups of the neon letters joined by hard cuts.
3. WHEN the close-ups end, THE Title_Sequence SHALL pull back for 12 seconds while the letters slide into place, so the full NOCTURNE NIGHT title is resolved 25 seconds after the start.
4. WHEN the title has resolved, THE Title_Sequence SHALL hold it on screen indefinitely with a gentle pulse, and SHALL fade in the Finale: the message "The lights are gone. Something heard you cross. The way forward is still hidden." and a Go deeper button.
5. THE Title_Sequence SHALL follow the wall clock, so that WHEN the page is hidden and shown again, it shows the frame for the current time, in step with the Arrival_Music.
6. WHEN the visitor presses Skip intro, THE Title_Sequence SHALL fade the current shot to black over 0.9 seconds, jump to 6 seconds before the title resolves, fade back in over 1.5 seconds, and finish the pull-back.
7. WHEN the title resolves or the visitor presses Skip intro, THE Skip intro button SHALL fade out and then be removed; IF it had keyboard focus, focus SHALL move to Go deeper.
8. WHERE the visitor prefers reduced motion, THE Title_Sequence SHALL show the resolved title, the message, and Go deeper at once, without animation.
9. THE Title_Sequence SHALL draw the title in red neon outlines only, with a soft bloom, light film grain, and a vignette, using Playfair Display.

### Requirement 3: Arrival Music and Sound Toggle

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want the music to start with the title, and to turn it off easily, so that the moment feels complete without forcing sound on me.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Other_Side begins, THE Site SHALL attempt to play the Arrival_Music once, for its full length, from the local file.
2. IF audible autoplay is blocked, THEN THE Site SHALL play the Arrival_Music muted and unmute it on the visitor's first click, tap, or key press that is not on the sound toggle.
3. THE Site SHALL show a round, icon-only sound toggle: a speaker with sound waves when the music is audible, and a speaker with a cross when it is not. It SHALL NOT show a text label or tooltip.
4. THE sound toggle SHALL expose the accessible name "Mute" with `aria-pressed` reflecting whether sound is off; WHEN the track has ended, its name SHALL be "Replay sound" and activating it SHALL replay the track from the start.
5. WHEN the visitor activates the sound toggle, THE Site SHALL stop automatic unmuting and honor the visitor's choice.
6. WHEN the visitor presses Go deeper, THE Site SHALL fade the Arrival_Music out, pause it, and hide the sound toggle.

### Requirement 4: Go Deeper Transition

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want stepping into the Underside to feel like the same crossing as before, so that the experience is consistent.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the visitor presses Go deeper, THE Site SHALL play the Invitation's glitch (pixel-block tears, sliding scanlines, brief hue shift) and its strongest shake on the Title_Sequence at the same time.
2. WHEN both have finished, THE Site SHALL turn the Title_Sequence over (rotate 180 degrees, shrink, blur, and fade) over 1.1 seconds and then start the Underside.
3. THE Site SHALL apply the shake and hue shift to the Title_Sequence element, not to `<html>` or `<body>`, so full-screen layers are not trapped by a transform.
4. WHERE the visitor prefers reduced motion, THE Site SHALL skip the glitch and shake and fade the Title_Sequence out over 1.2 seconds instead.
5. IF GSAP is unavailable, THEN THE Site SHALL run the CSS versions of the glitch and shake and continue to the Underside.
6. THE Site SHALL NOT request full screen when Go deeper is pressed.
7. THE Site SHALL start the Underside in Gentle_Mode exactly when the visitor prefers reduced motion; there SHALL be no separate Gentle_Mode button.

### Requirement 5: Standalone Title Video Page

**User Story:** As a visitor who opens `escape.html` directly, I want to see the title and continue into the experience, so that the old link still works.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN `www/escape.html` loads with motion allowed, THE page SHALL play the silent title video full-screen from local WebM with an MP4 fallback, using muted, inline autoplay.
2. WHEN the video ends, the visitor presses Skip intro, the video fails, or autoplay is rejected, THE page SHALL continue to `./index.html?descend`.
3. WHERE the visitor prefers reduced motion, THE page SHALL continue to `./index.html?descend` immediately.
4. WHEN `www/index.html` loads with `?descend`, THE Site SHALL skip the Invitation and begin the Other_Side.

### Requirement 6: Underside Structure and Movement

**User Story:** As a visitor in the Underside, I want a slow, deliberate descent through distinct rooms, so that the dread can build.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Underside SHALL present nine Beats in this order: hall, tape, nursery, eyes, rooms, leave, hallway, devil, exit.
2. THE Underside SHALL move the visitor deeper when they scroll up, and SHALL tell them so on its opening card.
3. THE Underside SHALL cap the movement speed at 1.15 screen heights per second, so fast scrolling never skips content.
4. WHILE a Lock is unsolved, THE Underside SHALL prevent scrolling past it.
5. THE Underside SHALL raise Dread from 0 toward 1 as the visitor goes deeper and SHALL never lower it; color (`--dread`, `--neon`) and the synthesized music SHALL follow Dread from red neon and arpeggios toward cold VHS tones and drones.
6. WHEN the visitor scrolls back after being told not to, THE Underside SHALL notice it, and the first time SHALL answer with a single-frame scare image.
7. THE Underside SHALL show a Leave link at all times that returns to the Invitation.

### Requirement 7: Locks

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want each room to ask something of me, so that escaping feels earned.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. **hall:** THE Lock SHALL open when the visitor clicks the host's portrait (Doré's Lucifer) in the 3D portrait corridor.
2. **tape:** THE Lock SHALL open when the visitor presses STOP three times and then EJECT on the VCR; EJECT before three stops SHALL answer "NO." and stay shut.
3. **nursery:** THE Lock SHALL open after the visitor winds the music box three times by holding the wind button (pointer, Space, or Enter); the doll SHALL change and come closer after each wind.
4. **rooms:** THE Lock SHALL offer three doors; the radio SHALL point to the left door, but only the right door SHALL open the Lock. A wrong door SHALL be disabled and bring the hammer (first time) or a gore scene (after that).
5. **leave:** THE Lock SHALL show a dialog asking "Are you sure you want to leave the party?" with two Stay buttons that only shake the dialog; its × SHALL dodge the pointer up to six times and then open the Lock when clicked.
6. **devil:** THE Lock SHALL ask the visitor to type the name they signed up with to cancel their RSVP. A wrong name SHALL bring "Wrong", the hammer, a gore scene, and move the Party_Clock 30 seconds closer to 3:00 AM.
7. **hallway:** THE Underside SHALL show the figure closer between cuts, then have it offer its severed arm with a Take it button before the devil Beat.
8. **exit:** THE Underside SHALL let the visitor escape by holding the escape button (pointer, or by holding Space); the hold meter SHALL fill over 2.6 seconds and drain over 5.2 seconds when released.

### Requirement 8: Party Clock and Time-Out Ending

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want a visible deadline, so that the descent has stakes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Party_Clock SHALL count down 10 minutes, shown as the time moving from 2:50 AM to 3:00 AM; Gentle_Mode SHALL add 2 minutes.
2. THE Party_Clock SHALL never go below zero and SHALL end exactly once.
3. WHERE `?time=SECONDS` is in the URL, THE Party_Clock SHALL use that many seconds, limited to 10 through 900.
4. WHEN the Party_Clock reaches zero outside Gentle_Mode, THE Underside SHALL announce "The party has ended." and play the finale: corner creep, severed-part rain, the cake stab scene, gore scenes, hands on glass, the Devil's claw, the hammer, a blood flood, and a final Jumpscare.
5. WHEN the Party_Clock reaches zero in Gentle_Mode, THE Underside SHALL show and speak the ending text only, without Jumpscares, and return to the Invitation.

### Requirement 9: Escape and the Outside Page

**User Story:** As a visitor who gets out, I want a clear ending, so that I know I made it.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the escape hold completes, THE Underside SHALL stop the Party_Clock, turn the house the right way up over 1.6 seconds, and show the Outside_Page with the heading "You got out.", the line "The party is still on. See you there.", and a Back to Nocturne Night link.
2. WHEN the Outside_Page appears, THE Site SHALL move keyboard focus to its heading and record `nocturne-night:escaped` in sessionStorage.
3. WHEN the Outside_Page has been visible for 6.5 seconds, THE Site SHALL show one final Jumpscare over it, outside Gentle_Mode.

### Requirement 10: Safety Limits

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want the scares to stay within safe limits, so that the experience is frightening without being harmful.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Underside SHALL pass every Flash through a flash limiter that allows at most 3 Flashes in any one-second window; the hammer SHALL count as a Flash.
2. THE Underside SHALL pass every Jumpscare through a scare gate that allows at most 2 per run, at least 45 seconds apart; only the time-out ending MAY force one, and never in Gentle_Mode.
3. IN Gentle_Mode, THE Underside SHALL show no Jumpscares, hammer, gore scenes, or Flashes, SHALL play quieter audio, and SHALL add 2 minutes to the Party_Clock.
4. THE Underside SHALL keep blood dark red and SHALL NOT produce bright full-screen red flashes.
5. THE Underside SHALL keep text (cards, prompts, timer, radio) above blood, cracks, and scares, and SHALL clear blood, cracks, and scratches within a few seconds.

### Requirement 11: Overlays and Sound

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want the house to react to my fear, so that it feels alive.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Underside SHALL pulse red corners at a fear-driven heart rate, with a matching audible heartbeat.
2. THE Underside SHALL drip blood from the top as Dread rises and splatter it wherever a splat sound plays.
3. THE Underside SHALL crack the screen where the hammer lands.
4. THE Underside SHALL synthesize its music and sound effects with the Web Audio API.
5. THE Underside SHALL play its 14 recorded voice lines from `www/assets/underside/voice/` through Web Audio with live processing, and SHALL fall back to browser speech synthesis when a clip cannot play.
6. IF Web Audio is unavailable, THEN THE Underside SHALL continue silently.

### Requirement 12: Content and Assets

**User Story:** As a developer, I want all copy and media to be original or properly licensed, so that the project is safe to publish.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Underside SHALL keep its copy in `www/js/content.js` (`underside`), and every broadcast card SHALL be eight words or fewer.
2. THE Site SHALL use only public-domain or CC0 images, or images generated by the team, and SHALL document every image, model, and audio file in `ASSETS.md`.
3. THE Site SHALL use original copy, puzzles, and visuals, without show logos, footage, character likenesses, or copied dialogue.

### Requirement 13: Performance

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want the Underside to stay smooth, so that the horror is not broken by stutter.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Underside SHALL lower canvas resolution when frames run slow.
2. THE Underside SHALL draw static noise from cached tiles, update `--dread` only in 2% steps, and animate the heartbeat corners with opacity only.
3. THE Underside SHALL avoid per-frame `shadowBlur`, per-pixel loops, and CSS variables that repaint full-screen gradients.
4. THE Title_Sequence SHALL draw its film grain from one cached noise tile moved by CSS, and its bloom from a quarter-size canvas blurred by CSS.

### Requirement 14: Local Data and Privacy

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want my details to stay on my device, so that the story can use my name without sharing it.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Underside SHALL read the visitor's name only from the `nocturne-night:signup` sessionStorage record, and SHALL use a generic name when it is missing.
2. THE Site SHALL NOT transmit signup details or progress to any server; network requests SHALL be limited to the Site's own files and the pinned library and font URLs.

### Requirement 15: Testing and Developer Tools

**User Story:** As a developer, I want the rules tested and easy to exercise, so that changes do not break the experience.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE project test command (`npm test`) SHALL run the Underside rule tests in `tests/underside.test.js` and pass.
2. THE tests SHALL cover at least: bounded, non-decreasing Dread; story-ordered Beats; the flash limit; Gentle_Mode never flashing or scaring; the Jumpscare cap and spacing; the hold meter range; the clock formats; card length; the capped scroll speed; the Party_Clock countdown; and that a Lock cannot be passed while unsolved.
3. WHERE `?debug` is in the URL, THE Underside SHALL expose `window.__underside` for automated walkthroughs.

### Requirement 16: Kiro Spec-Driven Development and Steering

**User Story:** As a developer, I want this feature defined through Kiro's spec workflow, so that its behavior stays reviewable and consistent with the invitation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Upside_Down_Escape SHALL be defined by `requirements.md`, `design.md`, and `tasks.md` in this spec directory using the Kiro spec format.
2. EVERY implemented feature in the Upside_Down_Escape SHALL trace to at least one acceptance criterion in this document.
3. THE Upside_Down_Escape SHALL follow the project steering (`product.md`, `tech-stack.md`, `code-conventions.md`, `design-language.md`, `underside.md`).
