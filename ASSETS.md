## Original work (this repository)
- All creatures (the Grinner, the eyes, the corridor figure, the neighbour at the door) are drawn procedurally in `js/underside/creatures.js`. They are original designs and do not depict any existing character, creepypasta image, sculpture, or real person.
- All other visuals are built in code: the invitation's moon scene, fog, candles, and glitch layers are CSS art (`style.css`); the NOCTURNE NIGHT title sequence is drawn on a 2D canvas (`js/title-sequence.js`); the portrait corridor, CRT room, cake stab scene, and gore scenes are rendered with three.js and WebGL; and the Underside's other set-pieces are drawn on a 2D canvas.
- All music and sound effects in the Underside are synthesized live with the Web Audio API (`js/underside/audio.js`). The only audio files are the arrival music and the voice lines above.
