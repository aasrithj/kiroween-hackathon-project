## Original work (this repository)
- All creatures (the Grinner, the eyes, the corridor figure, the neighbour at the door) are drawn procedurally in `js/underside/creatures.js`. They are original designs and do not depict any existing character, creepypasta image, sculpture, or real person.
- All music and sound effects in the Underside are synthesized live with the Web Audio API (`js/underside/audio.js`). The only audio files are the voice lines above.

- `audio/stranger-things.mp3` is not ours and is not licensed. Remove it before submitting; the rules forbid copyrighted music and third-party IP.
